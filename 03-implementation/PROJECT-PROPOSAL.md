## In plain terms (read this first)

Traffic lights at neighbouring junctions increasingly coordinate (green waves, ambulance preemption). This project puts a small AI (Phi-4-mini, running locally, never retrained) at each junction over a **trust-preserving coordination layer**. A classical controller (MaxPressure) stays in charge by default and acts as a safety net; the SLM is called in only on the hard, ambiguous cases, and separately writes a citation-faithful legal-reasoning note over the audit record.

The moment junctions act on each other's reports, a hijacked junction that still holds a valid key can lie, and a naive coordinator acts on the lie: wasted green, starved side streets, or a signal tricked into clearing the road for an ambulance that does not exist. A deterministic gate refuses a signed-but-uncorroborated emergency preemption from such an insider and clears physically-corroborated real emergencies; a signed hash-chained accountability log, anchored to a quorum of independent witnesses and read by a named cross-auditor, makes every decision reconstructable. That accountability mechanism is **not new — it is Certificate Transparency, credited to prior art and cited, not claimed as a contribution.**

The genuinely new result is a **measured characterisation** of exactly which stealthy insider deviations this layer can and cannot hold accountable, and one structural finding at its centre: the preemption attack controls the signal phase, which is *also* the variable that gates honest-witness coverage, so executing the attack opens the very coverage gap that would conceal it (the **self-referential coupling**). We do *not* claim the AI beats the classical controller on normal traffic (it does not, and the coordination effect is reported as an inert structural zero, never as a traffic-performance benefit). The honest boundary of what is accountable, measured on the real Euston Road (A501) corridor, is the finding.

Every specialist term below (MaxPressure, conservation check, CUSUM, BCa, GEH, and so on) is translated in plain language in [`GLOSSARY.md`](GLOSSARY.md).

## 1. One-line thesis

> An **on-device, trust-preserving coordination layer** for signalised junctions, and a **measured characterisation** of exactly which stealthy insider deviations it can and cannot hold accountable. A deterministic MaxPressure controller is the default and safety shield; a deterministic gate refuses signed-but-uncorroborated emergency preemption from a compromised insider and clears physically-corroborated real emergencies; a Certificate-Transparency-style, quorum-anchored, cross-audited accountability log (mechanism **credited to prior art, not claimed novel**) makes decisions reconstructable; and a frozen edge SLM is a self-contained co-equal contribution (a citation-faithful legal-reasoning note plus a guarded disambiguation classifier).

The three contributions answer one question: *in a compromised-insider emergency, what can be held accountable mechanically, and what remains a human/counsel judgment that even an on-device reasoner cannot faithfully automate?* They are: (a) the deterministic real-time gate; (b) the accountability log — whose mechanism is Certificate Transparency (RFC 6962 and successors), cited and **not** claimed as novel; and (c) the **novel object — the self-referential coupling**: the preemption attack controls the signal phase, which is also the variable that gates honest-witness coverage, so executing the attack opens the very coverage desert that conceals it. Stated as a conditional lemma under explicit hypotheses, with one severe, pre-registered measurement of its boundary on the real Euston corridor. The honest boundary, not an unconditional guarantee, is the finding.

The SLM is a **self-contained co-equal contribution, not the instrument of the boundary map** (whose scored origins are decided by mechanical provenance predicates): its primary job is a citation-faithful legal-reasoning note, scored on citation-correctness and false-citation-rate on novel legal fact-combinations against an un-rigged rule-to-text template; a secondary job is disambiguating a detector-flagged anomaly using cross-junction context. Its value is a **characterisation, not a competition**, and a pre-committed kill-criterion may demote the *claim* but never the SLM's presence. It is not claimed to beat a heuristic at routine phase selection (it does not, and it is slower).

## 3. Novelty (honest, reviewer-hardened)

**The claim that survives adversarial review (not "first" as load-bearing):** the **self-referential coupling** — the preemption attack lever (the signal phase) is also the honest-witness coverage gate — instantiated and *measured* on a real signalised corridor. None of the prior work below owns this coupling; adding a time index to a spatial observability result would be routine, the coupling is not. The accountability mechanism itself is Certificate Transparency (RFC 6962, CONIKS, A2M, TrInc, PeerReview, Küsters), **credited to prior art and cited, not claimed novel**. The finding is a measured characterisation of the free-deviation boundary: which stealthy conservation-consistent insider deviations escape, and where the phase-coupled coverage threshold sits.

**Why the gap exists (derived, not asserted).** Every prior coordinated system surveyed in Section 4 (CoLLMLight, CoLight, LA-Light, REG-TSC, EMVLight) consumes neighbour state or messages under a benign-environment assumption: no authentication, no plausibility screening on what a neighbour reports. From that starting point, three consequences follow by construction, not by claim. First, a single compromised peer's report propagates unchecked into a downstream control decision, because nothing in the pipeline distinguishes a true reading from a fabricated one before it is acted on. Second, emergency preemption amplifies the damage disproportionately: a single accepted lie does not just bias a metric, it commandeers green time and steals capacity from every other approach at the junction, so the failure mode is not graceful degradation but an acute, attacker-timed loss. Third, connected and coordinated deployments make the message channel a growing, physically consequential attack surface, since coordination is precisely the mechanism that turns one junction's compromise into a network-wide effect. None of the systems in Section 4 authenticate or plausibility-check what they coordinate on, so the gap is a direct consequence of that shared design choice, not an assertion made without support.

The gap decomposes into three independent axes, cross-checked against ten primary TSC systems in `06-literary-survey/ADVERSARIAL-FAILURE-SYNTHESIS.md`: no prior system simultaneously (i) coordinates multiple junctions, (ii) uses an edge language model for exception handling, and (iii) is robust to an authenticated-but-lying coordination peer. Each near-neighbour fails exactly one axis. CoLLMLight coordinates and uses an LLM but has zero input verification, with explicit trust-by-default in its prompt ("if the upstream or downstream information of X isn't mentioned, it means that they are in a good state with low occupancy"), so even withholding a report is an exploit. REG-TSC, VLMLight, iLLM-TSC, LA-Light and LLMLight use an LLM for exception handling but are per-junction and trust the EV or context signal outright. CoLight and MaxPressure coordinate on neighbour queues but do no language reasoning and trust those queues by construction; CoLight's attention mechanism learns higher reliance on high-influence neighbours, so a lying high-influence neighbour is weighted up, not filtered. EMVLight coordinates preemption but is RL, not a language model, and trusts the EV signal. SafeLight's safety layer guards action legality (collision-safe transitions), not input integrity.

This attack surface is not hypothetical. A single spoofing vehicle raised I-SIG's total delay by up to 68.1%, reversing the system's 26.6% benefit (Chen et al., NDSS 2018; Feng et al., Transportation Research Record 2672(1), 2018), and colluding vehicles defeat DRL signal control that, in the attack paper's own words, "fully trusts" that vehicles send true information (Qu, Tang & Ma, ACM TIST 14(6), DOI 10.1145/3625236, 2023; arXiv:2111.02845). The mature security literature does not close this gap because it defends a different adversary: blockchain-based TSC integrity (arXiv:1906.02628) gives non-repudiation, proof of who sent a message, not proof that the message is true; and residual-based detectors (Keijzer et al., arXiv:2104.03801) rest on formal detectability theorems that a stealthy, model-consistent insider can evade by staying inside the residual threshold. None of these treat a trusted, correctly-authenticated coordination peer that simply lies, which is exactly the insider this project targets.

Each individual axis is precedented; the **self-referential coupling plus its measured boundary** is the contribution, not new cryptography and not the accountability mechanism (which is cited prior art):
- The guarded fast-default / slow-LLM-exception architecture is **VLMLight** (arXiv:2505.19486). We extend it explicitly, citing it as prior art, to the multi-junction and adversarial-channel setting it does not address (it is evaluated single-intersection, with no inter-junction trust model; note its fast branch is RL-trained and it does handle emergencies at one junction, so our delta is the multi-junction + compromised-channel scope, not emergency handling per se).
- Multi-agent LLM neighbour coordination is **CoLLMLight** (arXiv:2503.11739). Its agents share neighbour state (read from a spatiotemporal graph, not signed messages) with no authentication or plausibility check, and it fine-tunes an 8B model; we authenticate and plausibility-check an explicit message channel with a frozen 3.8B model.
- LLM-as-emergency-exception-handler is **LA-Light** (arXiv:2403.08337), cloud GPT-4 on SUMO, tested up to an 18-intersection network, benign faults only (sensor outage, routine emergency); our faults are *adversarial and signed*.
- The closest *attack-detection on coordination* is **Keijzer2021** (arXiv:2104.03801), a control-theoretic sliding-mode-observer residual filter for collaborative intersection control. It does give formal (theorem-based) detectability conditions; our delta is the **self-referential coupling and its measured free-deviation boundary** (the recall-collapse point and the phase-coupled coverage threshold on the real corridor, Section 7), plus cryptographic identity and a tamper-evident, quorum-anchored, cross-audited accountability log it does not have. This is a measured characterisation, not a detection ROC.

**We do not claim** (these are commodity or precedented, and we say so): novel cryptography; that the SLM beats a heuristic at routine phase selection; that emergency handling itself is new; that a ledger creates trust. The "no-training edge SLM" is framed as a *constraint*, not a novelty (VLMLight also uses frozen local models).

**Positioning summary.** The unoccupied gap is derived, not asserted: it is the conjunction of a frozen no-training edge SLM as a guarded exception handler over a MaxPressure default+shield, where inter-junction coordination is cryptographically authenticated and screened by a vehicle-conservation plausibility check, evaluated in SUMO under a compromised channel. Every row in the table above that coordinates (CoLLMLight, CoLight, LA-Light, REG-TSC, EMVLight) does so on a benign-channel assumption, with no authentication or plausibility check on what is consumed; that omission is exactly what a single compromised peer exploits, and emergency preemption turns the exploit from a nuisance into a commandeered green (Section 3). The three closest threats are CoLLMLight (same coordination, but trusts shared state, fine-tunes), VLMLight (same dual-branch safety architecture, but 72B and no trust), LA-Light (same exception-handler role, but cloud and benign faults). The honest bound, stated from first principles: a conservation check can only flag a residual that leaves the plausibility band, so our layer stops unauthenticated spoofing and gross/supra-tolerance faults, but not a key-holding adversary (or ≥2-key collusion) that crafts conservation-respecting injections inside the band; that case is contained by revoke + audit, not detection.

The choice of a frozen edge SLM (Section 2, Section 8) is itself derived from this gap, not just a resource constraint (`06-literary-survey/ADVERSARIAL-FAILURE-SYNTHESIS.md`, Part B(c)). Systems that keep learning online (CoLight's GAT-DQN, SafeLight's residual RL, EMVLight's MA-A2C) expose a data-poisoning surface: a persistent insider can shift the learned policy itself, turning a transient lie into a permanent backdoor. A frozen model has no such path: its decision function is fixed, inspectable and version-pinned, so its behaviour on an adversarial test battery is reproducible and admits a safety argument the trained baselines cannot offer. That safety argument is structured, not informal: Goal Structuring Notation (GSN Standard v3, maintained by the SCSC Assurance Case Working Group) and AMLAS (Hawkins, Paterson, Picardi, Jia, Calinescu & Habli, University of York) are the assurance frameworks this project targets for arguing the frozen-SLM insider-robustness claim from adversarial-test evidence. The robust-degradation metrics in Section 5 (R1-R4) follow the resilience-triangle framing standard in CPS resilience engineering (degradation rate, residual/robust performance, recovery time) and Pullum's RL-specific robustness/resilience metric catalogue (arXiv:2203.12048). (The earlier multi-axis distributional-audit framing is NOT a contribution of this project and has been dropped; it is not reported.)

## 5. Architecture

**Tiered control (heuristic-first, AI-guarded), with two unifications.**

```
 default ─▶ MaxPressure (+ predictive green-wave coordination)   ← runs the predictable case; fast; throughput-optimal
              │  trigger fires? (conservation anomaly / emergency vehicle / incident / abnormal demand)
              ▼
 escalate ─▶ frozen SLM exception handler ──proposes──▶ deterministic shield validates / can override
              (invoked rarely, only on trigger)                  (safety envelope: min/max green, all-red clearance, anti-starvation)
```

- **Fast path (real-time):** junction A signs (Ed25519) a neighbour message carrying its release toward B; B verifies it against the permissioned registry and screens it with the conservation check before it can influence any decision; event-gated.
- **Trust path (async):** permissioned registry of approved identities with `revoke()`; vehicle-conservation reconciliation (claimed outflow vs observed inflow, adaptive band + CUSUM persistence); tamper-evident hash-chained audit log that anchors signed-message hashes (so an insider's lies are attributable to its key by an external auditor).

**Two unifications (the coherence of the project):**
1. The same signed `release` messages serve both integrity (conservation reconciliation) and coordination (timed platoon arrival for green-wave pre-positioning).
2. The conservation/anomaly detector that catches spoof/fault is **also** the trigger that escalates to the SLM, and the SLM's job on escalation is precisely to **disambiguate the flag**: a fixed rule cannot tell a genuine incident from a spoofed emergency, but the SLM can weigh cross-junction context (do neighbours corroborate the incident? is the claimed emergency physically consistent?) to decide whether to grant preemption or reject it. This is the one decision where the SLM is load-bearing; integrity and the AI-guard reinforce each other.

**Safety envelope (deterministic, independent of the SLM):** hard min/max green and mandatory yellow + all-red clearance on every transition (structural in the controller's `step()`, already built), plus an **anti-starvation override to be added to the shield** (track each approach's last-served tick; if any approach is skipped beyond `max_skip` decisions the shield forces it next cycle, an active emergency deferring it by at most one min-green+yellow). The shield clamps to this envelope; the SLM can never violate it. This is the formal safety claim, stronger than "MaxPressure vetoes invalid phases." Anti-starvation is the one envelope component not yet in the shield code path: it is a MUST build item with an `anti_starvation_violations` KPI.

### Formal system properties

The informal terms used above and in Sections 6-8 (robustness under compromise, escalation, the safety envelope) have a testable form. Parameters in `[K: ...]` are calibrated / pre-registered, not asserted.

**Robust degradation (testable systems property).** Under any single authenticated-but-compromised neighbour input (spoofed claim, inflated or under-reported release, replay, silence):
- **R1 Detection:** a deviation exceeding the plausibility band is flagged within `[K: D]` decision windows (measured as detection latency vs lie magnitude).
- **R2 Containment:** a flagged input's influence on control is zeroed, and uncorroborated preemption claims are refused at all times.
- **R3 Bounded degradation:** performance under attack stays within a pre-registered margin `[K: epsilon]` of the local-control (MaxPressure-only) fallback; the defended system never does meaningfully worse than simply ignoring neighbours.
- **R4 Safety invariance:** the safety floor (below) holds at every tick, attacked or not.
- **Honest bounds:** a within-band lie is undetected by construction (R3/R4 still bound its harm); in-band collusion defeats R1 and is out of scope (contained by revoke + audit, Section 6).

**Ambiguous case (deterministic escalation predicate).** A decision tick escalates to the SLM iff:
- **A1** the conservation/CUSUM detector flags a neighbour claim this window, or
- **A2** an authenticated emergency/incident claim has partial evidence (corroboration present but below the hard-accept threshold, or stale/inconsistent, e.g. a sighting older than the route travel-time tolerance), or
- **A3** two authenticated reports about the same edge contradict beyond the tolerance band.

Fully corroborated and zero-evidence claims are clear-cut: handled deterministically, never escalated. The SLM output is a proposal; the shield validates it against the safety floor.

**Safety floor (invariant set, SLM-independent).** The executed action always satisfies:
- **S1** min-green and max-green bounds.
- **S2** protected yellow + all-red clearance on every transition.
- **S3** anti-starvation: no approach skipped more than `[max_skip = 3]` consecutive decisions (the PINNED anti-starvation shield value per MASTER-SPEC §0 / §6.8; the earlier ⌈C_opt/Δ⌉=8 cycle figure is a looser upper bound, superseded by the tighter pinned 3; a corroborated emergency may defer it by at most one min-green + yellow).
- **S4** preemption only for corroborated emergencies.

If the SLM proposal violates the floor, the shield substitutes the deterministic choice.

## 6. Threat model (explicit)

**Attacker tiers (we state which we defend):**
1. **Outsider** (no key): forges/injects unsigned messages. Defended (auth rejects).
2. **Network adversary** (no key): observe/drop/reorder/replay on the wire. Replay is defended **within a session** (the bus dedupes `(recipient, sender, t)`); cross-restart monotonic persistence is a small, scoped change to the message bus (a persisted per-sender high-water-mark), flagged as a build item, not yet shipped. Drop/withhold is reconciled via silent-neighbour expectation.
3. **Single compromised insider** (one approved key): may lie within tolerance (undetected, stated), lie above tolerance (detected as an edge anomaly), or assert a fake emergency (the headline attack). Defended in the supra-tolerance and fake-emergency cases; the conservation check flags the edge, not the culprit (operational response: audit + revoke, with a guard against detector-driven DoS of an honest node).
4. **Colluding insiders (>=2 keys), conservation-respecting:** explicitly **out of scope**. By construction the colluders keep the mass-balance residual inside the band (one over-claims, another supplies a matching fake observation), so it is statistically indistinguishable from benign traffic and recall → 0. Contained by `revoke` + audit, not detection.
5. **Compromised admin/root key:** out of scope; the single point of total failure, stated plainly.

**Trust assumptions:** one honest city authority, one admin key (not compromised); registry is ground truth; per-junction private keys not exfiltrated; sensors honest-but-faulty (faults modelled; spoofing is the tier-3 case); clocks loosely synchronised (required for the replay window). Two further guards are **build items, not yet shipped**: a per-edge cap on consecutive spillback windows (added in the controller's reconcile loop, which is not frozen) that forces a reconciliation window through the CUSUM so an attacker cannot mask indefinitely behind the spillback classification; and a pre-verification payload size/field-count/depth bound so a valid insider cannot send an oversized signed payload. The detector flags an edge, not a culprit, so revocation is gated behind an **audit-before-revoke** policy with a per-neighbour flag-rate metric, preventing a forced flag from being weaponised to revoke an honest node.

## 7. The headline experiment: exploit, then defend (made fair)

The reviewer concern that "breaking an unauthenticated system is trivial" is addressed directly:

- **Victim baseline:** a cooperative controller **representative of the trust-everything class that CoLLMLight exemplifies** (CoLLMLight itself shares neighbour state via a graph rather than signed messages; we model the same trust assumption over an explicit message channel, which is the attack surface that class implicitly trusts). It is identical to our defended system **except** it omits signing, registry, and the conservation check, and is tuned to be **good in the benign case**. The defended-vs-victim benign delta is a **pre-registered acceptance criterion** (must be within the MDE / non-significant), and if the integrity layer's overhead does regress benign flow, that is itself a reported negative result rather than an assumed tie.
- **Adversary:** a single compromised-but-approved neighbour (tier 3), not an outsider. Attacks: (a) a signed over-claim/under-claim of release; (b) a **spoofed fake emergency** to commandeer preemption; (c) a within-tolerance/partial lie that the signed system must still reason about.
- **Break:** quantify the victim's degradation (network delay, wasted green on false preemption, induced gridlock, emergency-vehicle delay).
- **Defend:** signing blocks impersonation/replay; the conservation check (run through the **CUSUM detector we actually ship**, `flow_conservation.py`, not only the stateless checker) detects the inconsistency and the system **autonomously falls back to local MaxPressure on the flagged edge** (no human in the loop; we measure the traffic cost of this automatic degradation). On a flagged *emergency* claim specifically, the SLM disambiguates real-vs-spoofed using cross-junction context before any preemption is granted.
- **Scientific object:** the **measured characterisation of the free-deviation boundary** (a measured characterisation, not a detection ROC), operationalised as a *deviation-magnitude sweep*: the spoofer's over-claim is parameterised as multiples of the live adaptive band (e.g. 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0x band), x30 seeds, and we plot recall and detection latency vs band-multiple. The **recall-collapse point** (where sub-tolerance deviations evade), with collusion (recall=0) and spillback-masking marked as the bounding zeros, and benign false-alarm/false-preemption rate reported next to every recall number. On the real Euston corridor this becomes the headline object: the **phase-coupled coverage threshold** (the self-referential coupling), measured against a pre-registered well-mixed comparator.
- **Scope:** the deterministic-gate demo is exercised on the synthetic fixture (clean edge ids, known ground truth); the headline coupling measurement runs on the real Euston Road (A501) net via the committed `edge_map` path, with documented detector placement.

## 8. Evaluation

**Baseline ladder:** fixed-time → MaxPressure → MaxPressure + tuned rule-based preemption (the strong non-AI emergency floor) → naive cooperative SLM (the fair victim) → the defended SLM system. An RL reference (CoLight/EMVLight-style, trained offline in SUMO; training is allowed for *baselines*, only the deployed SLM is inference-only) is included for context, not as a target the SLM is required to reach.

**Performance, two-pronged and honest:**
- **Floor (reliable):** deterministic predictive coordination beats *vanilla* MaxPressure on delay where headroom exists (green-wave regime, literature-backed).
- **Characterisation (reported either way, not a bet to win):** on **detector-flagged anomaly states** (the one place the SLM is load-bearing, Section 5), the frozen SLM's ability to disambiguate real-vs-spoofed emergencies and reduce false-preemption / improve incident response is **characterised against the tuned rule as a reference**, not raced against it. The ambiguous test set is split into **anticipated** cases (the rule is tuned on these; parity is expected) and **novel/unanticipated** cases held out from rule tuning, where a frozen reasoner could plausibly generalise beyond fixed thresholds; the novel split also differentiates the SLM from trained-RL systems (Section 4), which need retraining to handle novelty at all. Answered by a **decision-attribution breakdown** (agree / disagree-better / disagree-worse / shield-vetoed) on flagged-anomaly states where rule and SLM disagree, reported separately for the anticipated and novel splits, plus a curated hard-state micro-benchmark with known-correct escalations. **Pre-committed headline decision rule:** if the SLM shows statistically and practically significant value on the ambiguous set, the SLM leads the contribution; if the result is null, the trust-preserving coordination layer leads (Section 1). Both outcomes are defensible and reported in full: a clean powered null ("a tuned rule suffices; the value of edge reasoning is bounded") is a *scoped, informative* finding, not an excuse, because the SLM is structurally central to this question either way.
- **Safety floor:** the guarded system never regresses normal flow vs MaxPressure (shield guarantee), measured.

**Selection-bias controls:** the substrate is the **real Euston Road (A501)** arterial with documented detector placement and demand calibrated to a named time-resolved source (TfL/DfT); always report the **full demand sweep** (scale 0.3->3.0) for every controller. The inferential target is this corridor's geometry (a quantified case study, geometry n=1); no "corridors in general" claim is made (Section 11).

**Determinism + reproducibility:** measure SLM temp-0 run-to-run agreement (N=100) on the **emergency/triggered prompts specifically** (longer and context-laden, where determinism is least guaranteed; the existing 100%-agreement evidence is only for the short phase-selection prompt), reported separately; only then memoise SLM decisions by prompt-hash for the eval so traffic variance is isolated from model variance; run the **real-SLM eval at n=30** (the StubAgent path structurally cannot show the effect; n=4 is underpowered); pin model hash, quantisation, Foundry version, decode params, seeds.

**Statistics:** BCa bootstrap + paired permutation + Holm, paired on seed (existing stack). Pre-register the primary metric family; report effect sizes with CIs as the headline and p-values as support; state the minimum detectable effect so a null is "below MDE = X," not ambiguous. Emergency vehicles treated as a clustered unit (random effect per seed), with enough injections per seed for meaningful n.

**KPIs.** Detection: precision/recall/F1, detection latency (cycles), false-alarm and false-preemption rate under benign jitter. Safety: shield veto rate, anti-starvation compliance, safe-but-suboptimal rate. Traffic (survivorship-safe, full-population tripinfo): mean network delay, completion rate, throughput, matched-set travel time; emergency AETT/AEWT with non-completion penalised and emergency completion rate; incident recovery time with a pre-registered censoring rule. Integrity overhead: sign/verify latency, ledger-vs-signed-log comparison.

## 11. Claims vs non-claims (the honesty boundary)

**We can claim:** message authenticity from a currently-approved member; tamper-evident, externally-auditable registration/revocation + decision/message log; detection of inconsistent (uncoordinated-spoof or faulty) neighbour reports with a measured envelope; a deterministic safety guarantee; a fair exploit-then-defend showing a competent cooperative controller is compromised and our layer defends it; an honest characterisation of whether a frozen edge SLM adds value on contested states.

**We cannot/do not claim:** novel cryptography; a novel accountability mechanism (it is Certificate Transparency, credited to prior art); that the accountability log creates trust or prevents lying (it records garbage faithfully; non-repudiation ≠ truthfulness); that the SLM beats a heuristic at routine phase selection; any traffic-performance benefit (the coordination effect is an inert structural zero); incentive-compatibility; which of two corroborating keys is the liar; defence against conservation-respecting collusion (≥2 keys), operator creation-time omission, or a compromised admin/identity-root key; absolute (vs relative-in-simulator) traffic numbers; external validity beyond SUMO and beyond this corridor's geometry (named as future work: hardware-in-the-loop).

**Critical limitations.** Four bound the contribution honestly, beyond the individual claims/non-claims above. First, the simulator-to-reality gap: every number is relative-in-SUMO; sensor noise, actuation latency, and real driver behaviour are not modelled, so absolute figures do not transfer, only the relative comparisons within the simulator. Second, scalability and external validity: the headline measurement is one real arterial (Euston Road, A501); the inferential target is this corridor's geometry (a quantified case study, geometry n=1), and network-wide behaviour at city scale is untested and named explicitly as future work (Section 9, NICE), not implied by the corridor result. Third, the single-trust-domain assumption: one city authority, one admin key, and a permissioned registry are load-bearing throughout; a multi-authority or federated deployment (mutually distrusting operators, e.g. adjoining authorities) is out of scope, and would need the full consensus-ledger option this proposal demotes to a cited-prior-art aside (Section 2). Fourth, what a null on the SLM characterisation would mean: if the frozen SLM misses the pre-registered citation-correctness/false-citation-rate bar on novel legal fact-combinations (Section 8), the pre-committed kill-criterion demotes the *claim* but never the SLM's presence, and the null is reported as a bound on where on-device reasoning helps, not a result to hide.

The safety floor and shield amount to a small safety-assurance case in the reliability-engineering sense: a deterministic, independently verifiable envelope (Section 5) is the assurance argument that the system cannot leave a safe state, with the SLM sitting entirely outside that trust base as an advisory proposer the shield may always overrule, so the reliability claim rests on the envelope, not on the model.
