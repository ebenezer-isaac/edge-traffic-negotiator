# COMP0234 marking criteria (authoritative, from Moodle, modified 2026-08-27)

Supersedes the generic UCL Level 7 bands used earlier in this project. The earlier
version had 70-85 and 86-100 as the two distinction bands. **That was wrong for this
module.** COMP0234 has THREE distinction bands, and the 70s band is the ordinary one.

Assessment: 90% dissertation, 10% viva. COMP0234 is 60 of 180 credits.

| Range | Descriptor | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| 90-100 | Exceptional (Distinction) | <1% |
| 80-89 | Outstanding (Distinction) | 2-3% |
| 70-79 | Excellent (Distinction) | ~20% |
| 60-69 | Good (Merit) | the majority |
| 50-59 | Satisfactory (Pass) | <10% |
| 45-49 | Borderline fail | |
| 0-44 | Unsatisfactory | |

## The five things every band scores

1. **Contribution and publishability.** 90+: "a clear contribution to the field, of
   publishable quality". 80-89: "potential contribution to field, could lead on to
   publishable work". 70-79: no contribution language at all, just "very well written
   report with a clear logical structure".
2. **Reading, critical thought, original interpretation.** 90+: "strong evidence of
   considerable extra-curricular reading, critical thought and original interpretation".
   80-89: "good evidence". 70-79: "demonstration of".
3. **Challenge and deliverables.** 90+: "challenging goals fully met, substantial
   deliverables, research level insight needed". 80-89: "challenging Project and
   substantial deliverables". 70-79: "a good level of challenge, substantial deliverables".
4. **Faults in execution and write-up.** 90+: "close to faultless". 80-89: "only very
   minor faults". 70-79: "some minor faults in execution or understanding".
5. **Independence and self-direction.** 90+: "a high level of independence". 80-89:
   "largely self-directed". 70-79: "a good level of self-direction". 60-69: "satisfactory
   self-management, with some supervision help needed occasionally".

**Criterion 5 was NOT in the criteria list used earlier in this project.** It is
explicit in every band here and it is a strength of this work: a six-week unattended
campaign on self-built, resumable, fail-loud harnesses, with the author diagnosing and
correcting his own methodology mid-campaign.

## Where the boundaries actually sit

- **70-79 vs 80-89** turns on two things: whether the work has a *potential contribution
  that could lead to publishable work*, and whether the faults are "minor" or "only very
  minor". Challenge and self-direction are already satisfied at both levels.
- **80-89 vs 90-100** turns on "close to faultless" plus an *actual* contribution of
  publishable quality, not a potential one.

## Read-across for this dissertation

Satisfied strongly: challenge, substantial deliverables, self-direction, extra-curricular
reading (40+ papers with theorem-level critique of MaxPressure), critical thought and
original interpretation (the authorship metric family, the saturation-masks-effect-sizes
methodological finding, the self-retraction of a headline claim).

Genuine claim to the 80-89 band's "could lead on to publishable work": the research
article exists in draft, and the authorship framing plus the saturation finding are both
novel to this literature.

What decides the band is therefore criterion 4, faults in execution and write-up. That is
what the section investigation is for, and why the visible TODO on the title page matters
out of all proportion to its size.

What would block 90-100 honestly: the headline effect is single-network and did not
replicate on the second calibrated map, the imitation-versus-rediscovery question is left
open by the author's own admission, and the work is simulation-only. Those are real and
disclosed, and "close to faultless" is not the same as "no limitations", but a clear
contribution of publishable quality is a high bar this does not quite clear.
