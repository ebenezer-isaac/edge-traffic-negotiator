\chapter{Experimental methodology}\label{ch:methodology}
% WORD BUDGET: ~1500 words (12k total target; COMP0234 limit UNVERIFIED — confirm on Moodle)

This chapter defines what I measured, how each comparison was controlled, and
the statistical decision rules I committed to before running the experiments.
The stance throughout is fail-loud: harness errors abort or are counted, never
silently scored, and null results are reported as findings.
