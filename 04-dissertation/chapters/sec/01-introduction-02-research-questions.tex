\subsection{Research questions}\label{sec:intro-rqs}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textbf{RQ1}] Can an on-device SLM ($\le$4\,GB, Foundry Local,
RTX~2060-class), behind a deterministic safety shield, match or beat
classical baselines (fixed-time, MaxPressure) on measured London
topologies in SUMO?
\item[\textbf{RQ2}] What does distilling a frontier teacher into the SLM
buy offline and closed-loop, and at what model scale does the benefit
saturate?
\item[\textbf{RQ3}] Can every actuation be bound into a tamper-evident
audit that, after a simulated collision, yields a UK-law-grounded evidence
pack rather than an unaccountable log?
\item[\textbf{RQ4}] Does a trust-coefficient mechanism over neighbour
reports withstand honest, blatant, and stealth misreporting?
\end{enumerate}
