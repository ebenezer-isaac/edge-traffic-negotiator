\section{Dissertation structure}\label{sec:intro-structure}
Chapter~\ref{ch:background} positions the work, and
Chapters~\ref{ch:design}--\ref{ch:methodology} give the architecture, the
engineering and the evaluation protocol. Chapter~\ref{ch:results} reports
the evidence in the order it was built, and
Chapters~\ref{ch:discussion}--\ref{ch:conclusion} answer the research
questions.
