\subsection{The fine-tuning axis}\label{sec:des-ft}
The final axis asks what changes when the proposer is fine-tuned rather
than stock. The pipeline is label distillation. A frontier teacher labels
junction states sampled from training-reserved seeds, and a second frontier
model re-labels four audit samples of the teacher's output (600 states,
98.5\% agreement, \S\ref{sec:imp-dataset}). A deterministic sanity
verifier screens the teacher's own answer rather than the state, rejecting
a label that serves an empty phase while other approaches are waiting or
names a phase outside the junction's range. The teacher is therefore
second-guessed on exactly two degenerate cases and never by a weaker rule
competing on the decision itself. The four prompt formats are rendered
from three separately sampled and labelled state pools into one mixed
training set; training is QLoRA \cite{dettmers2023qlora}, and the merged
model is compiled to weight-only int4 ONNX and served through the same
runtime as every stock model (\S\ref{sec:imp-dataset},
\S\ref{sec:imp-compile}). Three rules protect the comparisons
(\S\ref{sec:met-disjoint}, \S\ref{sec:met-baselines}): a holdout
hash-frozen before any training, closed-loop evaluation seeds disjoint from
training seeds, and a control arm of the same base model compiled through
the identical int4 pipeline, so the only manipulated variable on the 0.6B
axis is the weights; the 3.8B student has no identically-compiled stock
control. Ablation students, leave-one-topology-out and four format
specialists trained under the same recipe, test memorisation and
specialisation hypotheses.
