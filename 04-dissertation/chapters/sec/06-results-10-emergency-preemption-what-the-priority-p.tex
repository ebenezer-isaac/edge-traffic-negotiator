\subsection{Emergency preemption: what the priority path is worth}\label{sec:res-ev}
The authorisation machinery serves a concrete function, measured over
thirty seeds on the synthetic arterial corridor with a scripted ambulance
and spoofing attack in three modes, no preemption, naive and defended
(Appendix~\ref{app:ev-detail}, Table~\ref{tab:ev}). All four intervals
exclude zero: \finding{signal priority is worth about half a minute to an
emergency vehicle} (31.1\,s), \finding{security has a measurable price}
(8.0\,s to the ambulance), and \finding{that price buys quantified
protection} (4.6\,s mean and 16.1\,s worst-case cross-traffic harm avoided
under attack). These are per-event magnitudes from one scripted ambulance
and one attack window per run, not expected values under a naturalistic
call rate, and the baseline characterises no specific deployed system.
