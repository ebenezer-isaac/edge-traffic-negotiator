## In one paragraph (plain language)

A corridor of small language-model agents (Phi-4-mini, 3.8B, frozen, one per traffic junction) coordinate signal timing to clear ambulances and handle cross-junction incidents, on top of a classical **MaxPressure** controller that stays in charge by default and acts as a safety shield. The headline question is: **can a frozen, never-retrained edge SLM coordinate multiple junctions to handle emergencies and incidents better than isolated control?** The research question is not "can the AI beat the classical controller on normal traffic" (it cannot, and we say so). The secondary, deployability question is: when a neighbouring junction's messages are authenticated but compromised (a valid key in the wrong hands), how does coordination degrade, and how does it defend itself? The integrity layer uses signed channels, a conservation plausibility check, and a corroboration gate. Signing alone cannot catch an authenticated-but-lying peer; the conservation check and corroboration gate are what close that gap.

**Positioning.** Each axis is precedented; the unoccupied gap is the *conjunction*: a frozen no-training edge SLM as a guarded exception handler over a MaxPressure default+shield, where inter-junction coordination is cryptographically authenticated and screened by a vehicle-conservation plausibility check, evaluated in SUMO under a compromised channel. The three closest works are CoLLMLight (same coordination, but trusts shared state, fine-tunes), VLMLight (same dual-branch safety architecture, but 72B and no trust), and LA-Light (same exception-handler role, but cloud and benign faults).

## Table 2 — Why this project direction (decision record)

Each row is a decision reached through analysis and adversarial review, with the reasoning that held. The table exists so the direction is not re-litigated.

| Decision | Options considered | Chosen | Why (the reasoning that held) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Performance basis | SLM beats MaxPressure on normal flow; vs match; vs not a perf claim | **Not a headline perf claim** | MaxPressure is throughput-optimal and microsecond-fast; our own ablation shows Phi-4-mini reproduces its choice on the easy task and loses on the oversaturated grid. Racing the heuristic on its home turf is a dead end. |
| SLM role | Co-equal proposer; vs drop SLM; vs guarded exception handler | **Guarded exception handler** | Math-first: MaxPressure runs the predictable case; the SLM is invoked only on an anomaly/emergency trigger; the deterministic shield guarantees safety. Justifies the SLM without claiming it beats the heuristic. |
| Headline contribution | Emergency/incident coordination across junctions; vs robustness-under-compromise as headline; vs performance | **Coordinated multi-junction emergency and incident response** | Coordination alone is precedented (CoLLMLight); emergency handling alone is precedented (VLMLight, LA-Light, EMVLight); the fusion on a frozen edge model is ours. This is the primary claim. Robustness under a compromised channel is a strong secondary contribution: signing/ledger alone are commodity composition, and the unoccupied result is detecting an authenticated-but-lying neighbour via conservation check and corroboration gate. Robustness makes the system deployable; it is not the system. |
| Ledger | Hyperledger Besu/QBFT in the trust path; vs signed hash-chained log | **Signed hash-chained log; blockchain demoted to a justified-or-not aside** | Governance is a single city-authority key, so one trust domain and no Byzantine setting. A tamper-evident append-only log gives the same property. A ledger is justified only with ≥2 mutually-distrusting authorities; we say so. |
| Emergencies | Standalone "beat preemption"; vs cut; vs fuse into the security story | **Fuse: spoof-a-fake-emergency** | Standalone emergency handling is precedented and weakly novel. Spoofing a fake emergency to commandeer preemption fuses emergencies + coordination + robustness into one experiment that is genuinely ours. |
| Simulator | CityFlow (CoLLMLight's); vs SUMO | **SUMO** | Microscopic; supports emergency-vehicle dynamics (`vClass=emergency`) and lane blockage CityFlow cannot. Also differentiates cleanly from CoLLMLight. |
| Substrate | One synthetic corridor; vs real Lambeth; vs both + sweep | **Both + full demand sweep, pre-registered** | A single hand-built corridor invites selection-bias rejection. Pre-register the operating point against the green-wave literature; always report the full demand sweep and the real Lambeth arterial. |
| Identity | DID/VC; vs Ed25519 + permissioned registry | **Ed25519 + permissioned registry + revoke** | Full DID/VC verification is heavier than needed for a single trust domain; `proofmember23` itself proposes a lighter membership proof because VC verification is costly on constrained nodes. The registry suffices; DID/VC is future work. |

## Fact-check notes (what the 2026-06-20 audit changed)

The audit found **no fabricated papers**; every citation is real. It corrected over-attribution and a few specifics now reflected above: CoLLMLight is SFT (repo adds PPO), not "PPO"; LA-Light is multi-junction (18 Shanghai intersections), not single; REG-TSC and VLMLight *do* handle emergencies and use SUMO; SafeLight has its own rule-based safety override; the MaxPressure formula we implement is the simplified halting-count form, not Varaiya's full saturation-flow-weighted rule. A power-grid false-data-injection paper (Xiao & Weng 2026) that an earlier draft cited for a traffic bound was dropped; that bound is now argued from first principles. Full detail in `03-implementation/VERIFICATION-REPORT.md`.
