**We confirm the following:**

**1. Awareness of the Project**
The Coimbatore City Traffic Police has been briefed on the objectives, methodology and intended outputs of the above research project. We understand this to be a non-commercial academic study aimed at developing a fairness audit framework for AI-driven traffic signal control systems.

**2. Nature of Data Provided**
We [have provided / agree to provide] the researcher with the following on a voluntary and non-commercial basis:

- Aggregated historical vehicle flow counts (vehicles per hour per junction arm) from the signalised intersections along Avinashi Road (NH-544), from Anna Silai (Anna Statue Junction) to Chinniyampalayam Junction (approximately 11.6 km), for the period 25 May 2026 to 25 August 2026
- Signal phase timing parameters (green, amber and red durations) for those intersections

We confirm that the data provided is purely aggregate statistical information. It contains no video footage or photographic images, no vehicle registration or licence plate information, and no information that identifies any individual driver, pedestrian or vehicle owner.

**3. Data Protection**
We are satisfied that the data provided falls outside the scope of personal data as defined under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA) of India. No individual can be identified from the information shared.

**4. Non-Commercial Nature**
This collaboration is provided in the spirit of academic cooperation. It does not constitute a formal Memorandum of Understanding or legally binding data-sharing agreement, and no financial consideration has been exchanged or requested.

**5. Acknowledgement**
We have been informed that all research outputs arising from this project, including the submitted dissertation and any academic publications, will acknowledge the cooperation of the Coimbatore City Traffic Police.

**6. Report**
We understand that the researcher will provide this office with a copy of the completed audit report for the Avinashi Road corridor upon submission of the dissertation, expected by September 2026.
