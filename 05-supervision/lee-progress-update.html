The decision your feedback asked us to make

You both flagged that the SLM, our named novelty, was unevaluated, and asked us to test it against a well-tuned rule and decide the headline either way, including a clean null. We built the experiment and ran the real Phi-4-mini on Foundry Local. A first-pass prompt scored near chance (0.53); one round of prompt engineering lifted it to 0.74 overall, roughly halving its error. It still does not beat the tuned rule (0.89), so the defensible headline currently rests on the trust-preserving coordination layer, with the SLM characterization improving and ongoing. That is the experiment working as designed, not a setback.

04

Why a frozen edge SLM is the right call for Microsoft's story

It runs entirely on the edge (Phi-4-mini on Foundry Local, no cloud, no retraining) with verifiable per-junction identity, the same trust problem as Entra Agent ID, made physical. Freezing the model is a security and assurance advantage, not a limitation: a trained or continually-learning controller exposes a data-poisoning surface where a persistent lying insider can shift the policy itself, whereas a frozen model has a fixed, inspectable, version-pinned decision function that admits a reproducible safety argument.
