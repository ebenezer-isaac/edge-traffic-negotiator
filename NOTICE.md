# Third-party data and models

| What | Where | Source and licence |
|---|---|---|
| Street networks for Bloomsbury, Old Street and Euston Road | `sumo/bloomsbury/`, `sumo/oldstreet/`, `sumo/euston/` | © OpenStreetMap contributors, [Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/). The derived SUMO networks are also under ODbL. |
| Annual traffic counts (AADF) and A501 hourly counts | `sumo/euston/aadf.zip`, `sumo/euston/dft/`, `sumo/euston/a501_raw_hourly_counts.csv` | Department for Transport road traffic statistics. Contains public sector information licensed under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/). |
| Real-demand hourly profile for Euston Road | `realdemand/results/` | Derived aggregates of SCOOT vehicle counts: hourly totals summed over three on-corridor SCOOT sites (site IDs N02/015, N02/064, N02/144), per day for 12–14 May 2026 and as a three-day mean; the resulting hourly shares; each site's busiest-hour total; and the scaled hourly demand values (`frac`, `demand_vph`) recorded in each sweep result file. Released by Transport for London in response to an information request handled under the Environmental Information Regulations (TfL ref. 1453-2627). The raw per-detector SCOOT counts are **not** included. |
| MMLU sample (retention check) | `results/ft_dataset/mmlu_sample.json` | Hendrycks et al., *Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding*, MIT licence. |
| Base models | (downloaded at training time, not included) | Qwen3-0.6B, Apache-2.0. Phi-4-mini-instruct, MIT. |
| Fine-tuned weights | GitHub Release assets | Derived from the base models above and released under the same licences. |
| Claude reference-arm decisions | `realdemand/results/claude_replies.zip`, `realdemand/results/scoot_sweep_sonnet.json`, `realdemand/results/scoot_sweep_haiku_partial.json`, `realdemand/results/scoot_config_pilot_sonnet.json`, `realdemand/results/scoot_downstream_pilot.json` | Phase decisions generated by Anthropic Claude models (Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Haiku 4.5) through the Claude Code CLI, kept as the record of the real-demand reference runs. |
| Distillation labels | `results/ft_dataset/`, Release asset `ft_dataset_train.jsonl.gz` | Phase decisions generated by Anthropic Claude models. The `teacher` field on each row records which model labelled it. |

Full texts of third-party papers cited in the research notes are not redistributed; `research/papers/INDEX.md` lists them by arXiv ID.
