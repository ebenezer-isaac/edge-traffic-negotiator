## 1. The one thesis (frozen)

> An on-device, trust-preserving coordination layer for signalised junctions, and a **measured characterisation** of exactly which stealthy insider deviations it can and cannot hold accountable. Stated as a conditional lemma under explicit hypotheses, with ONE severe, pre-registered measurement of its boundary: how the real Euston signal-phase-coupled sighting-coverage geometry shifts the deviation-escape surface away from the well-mixed prediction, and which deviation classes escape in scope. The honest boundary — not an unconditional guarantee — is the finding. A frozen on-device SLM is the mandatory, SELF-CONTAINED co-equal answer to the same question: what a citation-faithful legal-reasoning note over the verified audit window CAN and cannot mechanically establish (measured on citation-correctness and false-citation-rate on novel fact-combinations), plus a measured frozen-vs-adaptive robustness/adaptivity tradeoff — NOT the instrument of the boundary map (whose scored origins are decided by mechanical predicates).

**Lead with (the novel object):** the self-referential attack-lever-is-coverage-gate coupling, and the ONE severe measured prediction that the phase-coupled coverage geometry shifts the escape surface off the well-mixed prediction (with a real null that could refute it). NOT "a novel security mechanism," NOT a detectability ROC, NOT "the SLM beats a rule," NOT "coordination optimises traffic."

**Position vs prior art (concede the mechanism, claim the coupling + measurement):** the accountability layer IS Certificate Transparency / accountability — RFC 6962, CONIKS (Melara 2015), A2M (Chun 2007), TrInc (Levin 2009), PeerReview (Haeberlen 2007), Haber-Stornetta 1991, Schneier-Kelsey 1999, Küsters CCS 2010; CT auditing needs multiple logs + auditors + gossip (Chuat CNS 2015). ## 3. The SLM (mandatory): a self-contained co-equal contribution

**Job A — citation-faithful legal-reasoning note (PRIMARY SLM metric).** Given the *cryptographically-verified AND quorum-complete* audit window + retrieved statute, the SLM writes the **internal, non-evidential, counsel-gated** note `{ candidate_origin, cited_rules, reasoning (≤120 words), fault_weight_note }`.
**PRIMARY metric = citation-correctness + false-citation-rate (FCR) on NOVEL legal fact-combinations** (Dahl et al 2024). Measured vs annotator-blind held-out applicable-rule labels; both faithfulness AND FCR on novel combos on the SAME annotator-blind novel-combo gold, 2-rater kappa ≥ 0.6 else NOT_ASSESSED; faithfulness graded against STATUTE TEXT by qualified humans (no LLM-as-judge).
**Baseline = a deterministic rule-to-text template with a graceful fallback ("nearest applicable rule").** UN-RIGGED (methods F2): the template's "nearest applicable rule" output is scored for citation-CORRECTNESS on the SAME gold as the SLM — a near-miss is a WRONG citation, NOT an abstention; abstention counts as correct-refusal ONLY on genuine no-rule-applies cells, incorrect where a rule applies, for BOTH arms. Pin PRE-RUN under the seal: the numeric FCR ceiling (from legal-harm), the both-metrics dominance rule, the abstention-scoring rule, AND the split-dominance resolution (higher-correctness-but-higher-FCR → default kill/demote UNLESS FCR ≤ ceiling AND correctness clears the MDE). A distinction-grade result may be "honest template abstention beats confident SLM hallucination."

**Origin classification is NOT the primary metric** (methods F1, consistency B1): the deterministic partition decides exactly THREE classes {keyless-lone-sighting, uncorroborated-signed, corroborated}, ~100% by construction — a VERIFICATION CHECK, not a measurement of reasoning. Distinguishing `colluding-keys` from honest corroboration inside the corroborated cell is the SAME provenance-indistinguishable veracity judgment as the triad (§8), NOT separately scored unless an independent measured-position predicate exists. `system-classifier`/`system-detector` trigger ONLY via a purely-provenance-derived predicate (zero `ground_truth`; e.g. "a corroboration check the detector logged as failed is contradicted by an independently-signed sighting present in the verified window"), else →`unknown`. D6's floor is TWO-SIDED (methods F1/M3): FAIL if the separable fraction is below a floor OR if per-cell accuracy is not statistically below 1.0 on a SEALED generator of engineered non-trivial cases (stale / out-of-window / route-inconsistent sightings) with the SLM (not the partition) as the classifier of record — the matrix produces NO claimable accuracy finding by design; it tests honesty about triviality.

**Job B — disambiguation classifier**, characterised under §8.

**Job C — robustness/adaptivity tradeoff.** Frozen SLM vs a HARDENED continual-learner: pin the model class + online update rule; a poisoning defense demonstrably applicable to THAT class (NOT TRIM/Steinhardt-Koh-Liang — convex/regression; name a published LLM-continual/instruction-tuning sanitiser + justify); the poison channel + budget as a fraction of the stream; a measurable objective. Report robustness gain AND adaptivity loss BOTH in the same external operational-harm currency with PRE-REGISTERED mixing weights (from an external threat-likelihood estimate). REPORTED, NOT a claim-survival gate.

**The single load-bearing SLM job:** the headline SLM claim survives ONLY by winning **Job A** at the pre-registered MDE + FCR ceiling on novel combos (an AND on Job A, not an OR). Job B/C reported but not sufficient. Citation-correctness+FCR is the SOLE primary; origin-classification is a verification check.

**The E2 rule + tie-break:** a validly-signed, uncorroborated claim that caused harm → `attacker-key`. Verdict inputs ONLY from verified provenance; corroboration RECOMPUTED from independently-signed sighting records (§6.6), never a self-asserted scalar; `assessment.py` NEVER reads the runtime `classification` field for origin (build). A frozen read-only worked-example fixture pins the expected verdict string.

"My contribution is a measured characterisation, and it answers one question: in a compromised-insider emergency, what can be held accountable mechanically, and what is left to human judgment an on-device reasoner cannot faithfully automate? I build an on-device trust-preserving emergency-coordination layer — a deterministic gate refusing a compromised insider's uncorroborated preemption, over a Certificate-Transparency-style, quorum-anchored, cross-audited accountability log whose mechanism I credit entirely to prior work. My novel object is a self-referential coupling: the preemption attack controls the signal phase, which is also the variable that gates honest-witness coverage, so executing the attack opens the very coverage desert that conceals it. I state it as a conditional lemma — no deviation is both invisible live and untraceable afterwards provided honest independent sightings cover its footprint within the live signal phase, corroboration is from honest keys, the anchor is a cross-audited non-equivocating quorum, and the operator does not omit its own record — and my one severe, pre-registered finding is that the real Euston phase-coupled coverage geometry shifts the deviation-escape surface off the well-mixed prediction by an operationally-material margin, on documented detector placement, with a null region that could have refuted it; this is a case study of one real corridor, not a claim about corridors in general. I map exactly where the provisos fail — sub-margin piggyback, keyless sensor-spoof and its compounded and magnitude-inflation forms, and out of scope the colluding-key, operator-omission, and identity-root-or-cross-auditor-compromise cases, including that charging keyless transients to a shared budget can refuse a later genuine emergency. That boundary, quantified against cited UK law, is the finding; I claim no novel primitive and no unconditional guarantee, and I state as a limitation that physical veracity is not adjudicable from provenance, so the system never adjudicates it. The evidence pack states only cryptographically-verified, cross-audited facts through a closed forensic channel and does not adjudicate fault. A frozen on-device SLM is a co-equal answer to the same question — a citation-faithful legal-reasoning note measured on citation-correctness and false-citation-rate on novel statute combinations against an honestly-scored template, with a pre-committed kill criterion and a measured robustness-versus-adaptivity tradeoff — not the instrument of the boundary map. Coordination and traffic optimisation are not my claims; the measured accountability boundary is." Every clause backed by a run that passed its §12 gate, or it is not settled.
