\section{System design}\label{ch:design}

A learned controller at a junction must be \emph{optional for safety and
mandatory for accountability}: the deterministic control path must be
complete without the model, and every actuation must be provable
afterwards, whoever authored it. This section presents the architecture
realising that premise and defines the three structural quantities
(authored share, proposal acceptance, divergence rate) that carry the
report's central finding.
