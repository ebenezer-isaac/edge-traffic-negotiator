\subsection{Motivation}\label{sec:intro-motivation}
Traffic signals are the most consequential actuators most cities own, and
the research frontier has begun handing them to large language models:
GPT-4-distilled controllers, RL-tuned reasoning models, cooperative agents
\cite{lai2023llmlight,wang2025trafficr1,collmlight2025}. Every one is
evaluated against a cloud endpoint, yet the junction is an edge
environment: no guaranteed backhaul, a hard memory envelope, and a
statutory duty, rare among LLM application domains, to account for itself
after a collision. This report takes the four research questions
(\S\ref{sec:intro-rqs}) together, since an accountability record concerns
the certified component only so far as that component authored the
behaviour. The work is conducted with and for Microsoft, which fixes the
serving stack, Foundry Local on a 6\,GB consumer GPU, capping the model
ladder at roughly 4B parameters; that constraint is the research object:
what \emph{this} deployable envelope can do, not what a datacentre could.
