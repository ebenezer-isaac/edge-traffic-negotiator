\subsection{The safety shield}\label{sec:des-shield}
The shield composes three mechanisms, named with the formal literature's
vocabulary (\S\ref{sec:bg-shields}). \textbf{Preemptive action masking}
\cite{huang2020masking}: the model chooses from a fixed menu of
conflict-free phases, so no proposal can create a conflicting green.
\textbf{Post-posed default substitution}: an invalid, unparseable or absent
proposal is replaced by the MaxPressure phase and recorded as a served
decision the SLM did not author. \textbf{Anti-starvation runtime
monitor}: a minimum-service floor force-serves any green phase passed over
for more than a bounded number of consecutive intervals, overriding both
the SLM and MaxPressure, except while an admissible emergency preemption
holds the signal. The floor is best-effort rather than proved. Only one
phase can be served per interval, so when two reach the threshold together
the code records the residue as a violation. This is a sound heuristic
composite, not a correct-by-construction shield in the Alshiekh sense
\cite{alshiekh2018shield}: there is no synthesis from a temporal-logic
specification and no minimal-interference proof, a limitation with an
upgrade path (\S\ref{sec:dis-future}). It still enforces, for every arm
including the baselines, no conflicting greens and no approach starved
beyond the floor except where the code records a violation.

Three measured quantities are defined on this structure, and their
denominators matter. Every logged decision tick is labelled by \emph{who
authored the phase actually served}: the SLM, a MaxPressure substitution,
or the anti-starvation floor.

\textbf{Authored share} is $\mathrm{slm}/\mathrm{decisions}$, the quantity
the audit argument needs: the fraction of \emph{logged decision ticks}
whose phase originated with the SLM. The denominator is the ticks the
controller logged. Those are the ticks where the congestion gate admitted
the SLM, plus gate-skipped ticks on which the anti-starvation floor changed
the served phase. Intervals the gate skipped without a phase change are
served by MaxPressure and counted on neither side. The ratio therefore
understates neither arm, but it is a share of \emph{decisions taken}, not
of wall-clock signal time.

\textbf{Proposal acceptance} is
$\mathrm{slm\_served}/\allowbreak\mathrm{slm\_valid\_proposals}$: of the
ticks where the SLM returned a well-formed proposal, the fraction served
rather than displaced. In practice the anti-starvation floor is what
displaces it.

\textbf{Divergence rate} is
$\mathrm{diverged\_and\_served}/\allowbreak\mathrm{slm\_served}$: of the
decisions the SLM authored, the fraction whose phase differed from what
MaxPressure would have chosen. It measures how far the served policy
departs from the classical reference, \emph{not} how often the shield
vetoed the model. Masking makes such a veto structurally impossible for
unsafe actions, and substitution fires on a negligible fraction of ticks in
the fine-tuned and stock 0.6B arms (\S\ref{sec:res-authorship}).

None of the three counts unsafe actions blocked, since the substitution
path catches only malformed or absent output. Reporting all three
separately prevents the error of reading a high acceptance rate as a high
share of authorship.
