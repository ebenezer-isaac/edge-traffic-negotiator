\subsection{Evaluation axes and metrics}\label{sec:met-metrics}
I evaluate along four axes. \textbf{Closed-loop control quality.} The
primary metric is mean network delay: the mean, over all vehicles that
\emph{departed} into the network, of total time in network (SUMO's
tripinfo \texttt{duration}) over a fixed 1,200-step horizon, a completed
trip contributing its full travel time and a vehicle still running at the
horizon the time it has accrued. Every controller cell is paired with a
fixed-time baseline on the \emph{same topology and seed}, so the reported
quantity is the paired relative delay change; completed-vehicle
throughput, teleport count and undeparted backlog guard against delay
``improved'' by starving demand. \textbf{Offline decision quality.}
Accuracy against frozen frontier-teacher labels on a held-out set of
junction states at int4 precision exactly as served, plus strict-JSON rate
(responses matching \texttt{\{"phase": N\}} exactly) and parse rate (any
recoverable phase). \textbf{Structural authorship.} Authored share,
proposal acceptance and divergence rate (\S\ref{sec:des-shield}), reported
alongside the decision composition itself. \textbf{Serving viability.}
Decision latency (p50/p99) against the 10\,s decision interval, and a log
of serving-reliability events with their mitigations.
